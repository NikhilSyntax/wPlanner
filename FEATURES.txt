================================================================================
wPlanner (Worship Planner) - Application Features Overview
================================================================================

wPlanner is a collaborative church service planning, worship management, and volunteer scheduling platform. Below is a brief and precise summary of its core features:

1. AUTHENTICATION & CHURCH TENANCY
   - Secure user registration, JWT-based authentication, and profile management.
   - Multi-church organization with an administrator approval workflow for new members.
   - Role-based access control (Admin, Leader, Member).

2. EVENT & SERVICE PLANNING
   - Service Scheduling: Create, edit, and organize church events and services.
   - Setlist Builder: Attach songs, assign performance keys, and structure song sequences.
   - Production Planning: Organize service orders, cue timelines, item durations, and production notes.
   - Volunteer Scheduling: Assign team members to event roles and track confirmation statuses (Pending, Accepted, Declined).

3. WORSHIP SONG REPOSITORY
   - Song Library: Manage songs with lyrics, chord sheets, default keys, tempo (BPM), themes, and external links.
   - Usage Analytics: Track song usage frequency and performance history across events.
   - Musician View: Optimized in-event setlist interface for viewing lyrics and chords during rehearsals and live services.

4. TEAM MANAGEMENT
   - Team Organization: Create specialized ministry teams (e.g., Worship, Audio/Visual, Ushers, Media).
   - Roster Management: Assign team leaders and organize volunteer rosters by skill/position.

5. REAL-TIME COMMUNICATION
   - Dedicated Chat Rooms: Real-time messaging for specific events and teams powered by WebSockets (Socket.IO).
   - Instant updates and coordination between leaders and team members.

6. NOTIFICATIONS & ALERTS
   - In-app notification center for schedule changes, assignment requests, and team updates.
   - Web Push Notifications: Browser-level push support for immediate volunteer alerts.

7. INTERACTIVE DASHBOARD
   - Centralized dashboard displaying upcoming services, personal roster assignments, quick-access setlists, and notification summaries.
================================================================================
