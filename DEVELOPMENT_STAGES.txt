STAGE 1: VERSION 1 - EVENT CREATION & AUTOMATED REMINDERS
================================================================================

[Primary Objective]
Establish the foundational event management system that enables leaders to schedule
worship services and ensures every assigned volunteer and team member receives timely,
automated reminders across multiple communication channels.

[Key Features]
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Service & Event Scheduling:
   - Create, edit, publish, and manage worship events and services.
   - Configure event metadata: Title, Event Type (Sunday Service, Midweek, Youth, etc.),
     location, description, start date/time, and end date/time.
   - Event status lifecycle: Draft -> Published -> Completed / Cancelled.

2. Multi-Tier Advance Reminders:
   - Automated time-based reminder triggers:
     * 24 Hours in Advance: Next-day preparation alert.
     * 12 Hours in Advance: Half-day countdown alert.
     * 2 Hours in Advance: Final call and check-in alert.
   - Manual broadcast reminder button for instant leader-to-team notifications.

3. Multi-Channel Notification Dispatch:
   - In-App Notification Center: Persistent badge and notification tray.
   - Real-Time WebSocket Alerts: Instant notification pops for active users.
   - Web Push Notifications: Chrome / browser background push banners (via VAPID/Web-Push)
     even when the user does not have the app tab active.

4. Audience Targeting & De-duplication:
   - Reminders automatically target all assigned volunteers and team members.
   - Action initiators / senders are excluded from receiving their own trigger alerts.

[Operational Workflow]
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Step 1: Church leader logs in and navigates to "Create Event".
Step 2: Leader inputs service details (Title, Type, Date, Time, Description) and publishes.
Step 3: Background Scheduler (Cron/Timer) runs periodically in the backend:
        - Evaluates upcoming scheduled start times against 24h, 12h, and 2h windows.
        - Identifies assigned members and team recipients.
Step 4: Notifications are dispatched through in-app channels and Web-Push services.
Step 5: Volunteers tap/click the notification, which deep-links directly to the Event Overview.

STAGE 2: VERSION 2 - COLLABORATION, TEAMS & REAL-TIME COMMUNICATION
================================================================================

[Primary Objective]
Transform single-user planning into a collaborative team ecosystem with role-based
access control, volunteer scheduling/opt-in confirmations, and real-time chat.

[Key Features]
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Multi-Tenant Church & Team Management:
   - Multi-church organizational partitioning.
   - Dedicated Ministry Teams (Worship Team, Tech/Production, Ushers, Hospitality, Media).
   - Team member directories with designated positions, skills, and contact info.

2. Role-Based Access Control (RBAC):
   - Super Admin: System-wide oversight.
   - Church Admin: Church configuration, user approval, team creation, full permissions.
   - Worship Leader / Event Manager: Event planning, setlist building, roster assignments.
   - Volunteer / Member: View assigned events, accept/decline invites, access charts & chat.

3. Volunteer Roster & Assignment Workflow:
   - Assign volunteers to specific service roles (Worship Leader, Acoustic, Electric,
     Bass, Drums, Keys, Vocals, Sound Tech, Media/Slides, Usher).
   - Real-time RSVP / Confirmation status: Pending -> Confirmed / Declined / Substitute Requested.
   - Conflict warnings if volunteers are double-booked.

4. Real-Time Chat & Discussion:
   - Event-Specific Chat: Dedicated channel per service for rehearsal notes, run-throughs,
     and prayer requests.
   - Team Chat: Persistent communication hubs for ongoing department discussions.
   - Real-time socket delivery, unread message badges, and sender avatars.

[Operational Workflow]
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Step 1: Church Admin creates teams and invites/approves volunteer user accounts.
Step 2: Event Planner opens the "Team Build / Assignments" tab of an event.
Step 3: Planner assigns volunteers to roles and publishes the roster.
Step 4: Volunteers receive instant assignment notifications and RSVP (Accept / Decline).
Step 5: Team members open the Event Chat to collaborate on rehearsal timelines, key changes,
        and service flow in real time.

STAGE 3: VERSION 3 - SONG BANK & INTERACTIVE SETLIST INTEGRATION
================================================================================

[Primary Objective]
Centralize the church's musical repertoire and integrate dynamic setlist planning directly
into events, enabling seamless musician rehearsals and live stage execution.

[Key Features]
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Centralized Song Bank:
   - Master repository for church songs.
   - Metadata: Title, Artist, Original Key, BPM / Tempo, Time Signature, CCLI #, Themes.
   - Rehearsal Assets: Lyrics, Chord Charts, YouTube/Spotify reference links, audio files.

2. Interactive Event Setlist Builder:
   - Search and attach songs from the Song Bank to specific events.
   - Drag-and-drop / ordered worship sequence (Opener, Praise, Worship, Offertory, Response).
   - Event-specific custom key overrides (e.g., Original: D -> Sunday Key: Bb).
   - Assign specific Vocal Leaders or Lead Instrumentalists per song.
   - Transition & performance notes (e.g., "Acoustic intro only", "Modulate on Bridge").

3. Live Musician View & Transposition:
   - Stage-friendly digital setlist view for mobile devices, tablets, and desktops.
   - Formatted chord sheet presentation with dynamic transposition.

4. Song Usage & Rotation Tracking:
   - Tracks total times sung and last date used across historical events.
   - Helps worship leaders avoid song over-repetition and re-introduce classic anthems.

[Operational Workflow]
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Step 1: Music Director populates the Song Bank with songs, default keys, chords, and links.
Step 2: Worship Leader opens an upcoming Event and goes to the "Setlist" tab.
Step 3: Leader searches the Song Bank, adds 4-6 songs, sets keys, and assigns vocal leads.
Step 4: Musicians receive notification of the published setlist and open the Event Setlist view
        to review chord sheets and listen to YouTube/audio reference links.
Step 5: During Sunday rehearsal/service, musicians read chords and lyrics directly in the app.
Step 6: Upon event completion, song usage metrics (last played date, usage count) update automatically.

STAGE 4: VERSION 4 - STATISTICS, ANALYTICS & INTELLIGENT AUTOMATION
================================================================================

[Primary Objective]
Leverage collected platform data to provide actionable ministry health analytics and
introduce intelligent automation for recurring event generation and roster scheduling.

[Key Features]
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Ministry Analytics & Health Metrics:
   - Volunteer Participation Analytics:
     * Individual serving frequencies, total services per month/quarter.
     * Acceptance / decline rates and response time benchmarks.
     * Volunteer burnout alerts (warning if a volunteer is scheduled too many consecutive weeks).
   - Repertoire & Song Analytics:
     * Most played songs, least played songs, dormant songs (> 90 days unused).
     * Song theme distribution (Praise vs Worship vs Communion vs Fast/Slow BPMs).
   - Ministry Growth & Attendance Metrics:
     * Service counts, team coverage percentage, unfilled role alerts.

2. Auto-Event Scheduler & Recurring Templates:
   - Create recurring schedule rules (e.g., "Every Sunday at 9:00 AM & 11:15 AM", "Every 1st Wednesday").
   - Define template parameters: Default Service Type, Title format, Duration, and Assigned Teams.
   - Rolling Advance Generation: Backend automatically generates events 2 to 6 weeks in advance.
   - Integrated lifecycle: Auto-generated events inherit multi-tier 24h/12h/2h reminders automatically.

3. Intelligent Roster & Auto-Assignment Engine:
   - Smart volunteer rotation based on past serving frequency and availability preferences.
   - Automated conflict resolution preventing cross-team scheduling clashes.

[Operational Workflow]
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Step 1: Church Admin configures an "Auto-Schedule Template" (e.g. Weekly Sunday Worship Service).
Step 2: Automated backend worker evaluates schedule rules and auto-provisions upcoming weekly events.
Step 3: Platform populates default team assignments and schedules notification reminders.
Step 4: Church leadership views the Ministry Dashboard to analyze monthly trends:
        - Identify heavily burdened volunteers to rotate off.
        - Discover under-utilized songs to refresh Sunday setlists.
        - Verify overall team health, attendance readiness, and fill open volunteer spots.

STAGE 5: VERSION 5 - LIVE LYRICS & CHORDS TV / OPERATOR PRESENTATION SYSTEM
================================================================================

[Primary Objective]
Provide an authoritative live presentation system separating the Operator Control Console
(laptop/tablet) from the Viewer Display (church TV, projector, stage monitor) with
strict 2-line windowing, character-anchored chord positions, and sub-second WebSocket sync.

[Key Features]
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Authoritative Live Presentation Architecture:
   - Backend LiveSession as single source of truth across browser refreshes.
   - Dedicated Live Operator Console (/live/operator/:eventId).
   - Dedicated Pure Viewer Presentation Screen (/live/viewer/:token).
   - 4-Digit Display Pairing Code workflow with persistent display tokens.

2. Distance-Optimized Strict 2-Line Windowing:
   - Always displays exactly TWO lyric lines maximum at any given time.
   - No continuous scrolling; whole-slide transitions on NEXT / PREV.
   - Center-aligned, ultra-readable typography optimized for 1080p and 4K TVs.

3. Character-Anchored Chord Alignment:
   - Chords structurally anchored to exact character positions in the lyric text.
   - Resilient against zoom, font size variations, and screen aspect ratio changes.
   - Toggle Chords ON/OFF with dynamic transposition.

4. Operator Control Console & Live Previews:
   - Real-time Current Live Output (exact 2 lines on TV).
   - Next Slide Preview (upcoming 2 lines or upcoming song section).
   - Structured Section Switcher ([Intro], [Verse 1], [Chorus], [Bridge], etc.).
   - Instant Blackout Screen (B) and Clear Screen (C) modes.
   - Real-time connected display counter and connection status badge.

[Operational Workflow]
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Step 1: Worship Leader / Admin opens the Event Setlist and clicks "Present Live (TV)".
Step 2: TV or Projector opens /live/viewer and enters the 4-digit display pairing code.
Step 3: TV pairs, remembers display token, and shows the live service state.
Step 4: Operator presses NEXT / Space or selects sections to advance lyrics & chords 2 lines at a time.
Step 5: Viewers update instantly via WebSockets without page reload.
